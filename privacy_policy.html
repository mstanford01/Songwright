Songwright — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

The short version: Songwright doesn't collect, store, or transmit any of your data anywhere. Everything — your dictionary lookups, your song ideas, your recordings — stays on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no network connections at all.

What Songwright does on your device

Songwright's dictionary, rhyme, and thesaurus lookups run against a database bundled inside the app itself. Your searches are never sent anywhere — there's no server to send them to.

Song ideas, lyrics, and quick-take recordings you create are stored locally in the app's private storage on your device, using Apple's standard on-device frameworks (SwiftData and the file system). Songwright has no server component and does not sync this data anywhere, including iCloud, unless a future version explicitly adds and discloses that.

Microphone access

Songwright asks for microphone permission to power two features:

  • Tuner — analyzes the pitch of sound picked up by the microphone, live, entirely on your device. No audio is recorded or saved by this feature.
  • Quick-take recordings — records short audio clips you explicitly start and stop, attached to a song idea you create. These recordings are saved only in the app's local storage on your device.

No audio, transcript, or derived data from either feature ever leaves your device.

Data we collect

None. Songwright has no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no advertising network, and no third-party services of any kind integrated into it. We do not know who our users are, how they use the app, or anything else about them.

Data sharing

Not applicable — since Songwright doesn't collect any data, there is none to share, sell, or disclose to anyone, including us.

Children's privacy

Because Songwright collects no data from any user, it does not collect data from children either.

Third-party content

Songwright's dictionary and rhyme data are derived from Princeton WordNet and the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary, both bundled locally and used under their respective open licenses. See the "Acknowledgments" screen inside the app for full attribution text.

Changes to this policy

If Songwright's data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect it.
