Common questions

Why didn't a word show up in the dictionary, rhymes, or thesaurus?
Songwright's word data comes from WordNet and the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary, both bundled on your device. They cover a very large vocabulary, but very new slang, proper nouns, or obscure terms may not be included yet.

The tuner isn't picking up my guitar — what should I check?
Make sure Songwright has microphone access (Settings → Songwright → Microphone), play a single string cleanly, and try to reduce background noise. The tuner needs a moment to lock onto a steady pitch.

Where are my song ideas and recordings stored?
Entirely on your device — Songwright has no account system and no server. Nothing you write or record leaves your phone.

Does Songwright work without an internet connection?
Yes. Every feature — dictionary, rhymes, thesaurus, chord tools, tuner, and idea capture — works fully offline.
